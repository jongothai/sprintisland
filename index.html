Welcome to Sprint Island, where you come not just to solve problems, but to unwind, bond with your team, and find your creative feet in a hands on 10 day design sprint experience. A design sprint is a structured problem solving process used by product and design teams to quickly go from a challenge or question to a tested idea without building a full product first.
